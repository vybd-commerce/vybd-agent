# Collaboration — user stories

TL;DR: Friends build one brand together. The owner sends an invite link, each member owns a lane, and everyone shares one thread and one Brand Brain with the agent. Milestone 8, not built yet. Done when two accounts share a brand, each claims a lane, and the agent flags an unowned step.

| # | As a… | I want to… | So that… | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Owner | Invite friends with a link (header → Invite) | We build the brand together | Invitee signs up via the link and lands in the brand as a member; link expires and works once |
| 2 | New member | Tell the agent what I bring when I join | My part is recorded | Agent asks, then saves a lane (name, owner, one-line commitment) shown in the Team tab |
| 3 | Member | Talk in one shared thread with the team and the agent | We stay in sync | Messages show author + avatar; agent answers in my lane's context, stays quiet unless @vybd or it spots a problem |
| 4 | Lane owner | Get my commitment turned into a short checklist | I know what to do next | Agent writes 2–4 tasks per lane; only the owner ticks them; everyone sees progress |
| 5 | Team | Be told when a needed step has no owner | Nothing falls through | Gap callout "Nobody owns this yet" with chips: claim it, quote a job, find someone |
| 6 | Owner | Be the only one who approves and pays for jobs | Spend stays under control | Any member can request a quote; Approve shows only for the owner |
| 7 | Owner | Remove a member without losing their work | The brand keeps its history | Their messages stay; their lane moves to the owner; every brief and lane change keeps its author |

Not in v1: real-time co-editing of the brief, equity/splits, private lane threads, email or WhatsApp notifications.
